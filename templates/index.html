Health — contactless vitals monitoring

How Wi-Fi signal ripple becomes a breathing readout while you sleep.

Classification pipeline

What happens physically

A sleeping person's chest rises and falls roughly every 2–6 seconds. Those few millimetres of movement subtly shift the multipath reflections of the Wi-Fi signal, which shows up as a slow periodic ripple across the CSI amplitudes. The model isolates that ripple with a band-pass filter over 0.1–0.5 Hz (6–30 breaths/min) and confirms it only when overall motion variance is near zero — still body, live lungs.

Decision logic (Python)

# per ~30 s window of subcarrier amplitudes
window = csi_series[-30:] - mean(csi_series[-30:])
ripple = band_power(window, f_lo=0.1, f_hi=0.5)
var    = variance(window)

if var < VAR_QUIET and ripple > RIPPLE_MIN:
    classify(STATE_SLEEPING)
    lock_vitals()   # → blue "Monitoring Vitals" badge

Breathing-band power

Spectral energy inside 0.1–0.5 Hz must dominate the residual noise.

Autocorrelation peak

A repeating period in the ripple confirms respiration over random drift.

Quiet-baseline variance

Near-zero motion variance proves the subject is stationary, not walking.

Security — motion detection & intruder alerts

How amplitude spikes become an instant intrusion alarm.

Classification pipeline

What happens physically

A person moving through the room crosses many multipath paths at once. Each crossing detunes dozens of subcarriers simultaneously, so the amplitude series erupts with wide, erratic spikes — very unlike the gentle periodic ripple of breathing or the flat line of an empty room. The model watches a short rolling window; when aggregate energy bursts past the motion threshold it flips to STATE 1 · MOVING. If Away Mode is armed at that instant, the alert path fires: a red toast, a flashing border on the status card, and an event in the log.

Decision logic (Python)

sigma2 = variance(csi_window)          # total disturbance energy
spikes = count(abs(amp) > 3 * mad)     # outlier subcarriers

if sigma2 > SIGMA_MAX or spikes > N_SPIKES:
    classify(STATE_MOVING)
    if away_armed:
        raise_intruder()   # socket event → toast + red flash

Amplitude variance σ²

Aggregate burst energy across all 64 subcarriers.

Spike rate vs MAD

Count of subcarriers exceeding three median absolute deviations.

RSSI drop-throughs

Sudden link-quality dips as a body absorbs the direct path.

Eco — occupancy-driven energy savings

How the classifier's output becomes lower bills and idle kWh banked.

Actuation pipeline

Why it saves energy

HVAC and lighting are usually run on fixed schedules that ignore reality — cooling an empty house, lighting an empty kitchen. WifiSense replaces the schedule with presence: the moment the classifier reports Empty, automation powers devices down and the meter starts banking the avoided draw. Sleep mode drops HVAC to an efficient low setpoint instead of full comfort. Manual overrides always win until you restore Auto.

Automation policy (Python)

if state == EMPTY:
    hvac.off(); lights.off()
    meter += dt * IDLE_KW      # counter ticks here only
elif state == SLEEPING:
    hvac.set(temp=20); lights.off()   # low-power mode
else:  # MOVING
    hvac.set(temp=22); lights.set(brightness=80)
